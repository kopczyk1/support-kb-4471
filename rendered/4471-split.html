#4471 is not one bug. It holds three problems with three different fixes. One fix will not close the ticket.

The ticket says: the parser reads dates month first, our tenants write day first. That is true for most of the 412 reports. It is not true for all of them.

What is inside

412 reports, 3 problems

  1. parser: 374 reports, 11 tenants. The shared parser reads every date month first. Day-first tenants see day and month swapped (7 April becomes 4 July). This is the cause the ticket names.
  2. hint: 33 reports, 3 tenants (alder.physio, copper.studio, pale.harbour). These tenants run booking-v3. Its form prints MM/DD/YYYY under the date field, so day-first people type month first. The parser is not at fault. The hint is. Fixing the parser does not help them, and it can make them worse.
  3. oneDay: 5 reports, 5 tenants. Not a swap. The date is exactly one day off (Tuesday 12 May shows as Monday 11 May). The desk flagged these in desk-notes.json: R-41202, R-41209, R-41236, R-41344, R-41620. The reports hint at a time zone or daylight saving error, not at a parser error.

How we counted

  • The export has 427 rows but 412 unique ids. We count each id once.
  • We placed each report by its tenant (locale and booking form), not by intakeTag. Intake guesses from the reporter's browser. It tagged 23 parser reports as hint and 15 hint reports as parser.
  • The five one-day reports were all filed under parser by tenant. We pulled them out.

What to do

  • Keep #4471 for parser only.
  • Open one ticket for hint and one for oneDay.
  • Do not tell the 33 booking-v3 reporters that a queued parser fix covers them.
